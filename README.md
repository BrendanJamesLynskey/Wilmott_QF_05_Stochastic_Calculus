# Elementary Stochastic Calculus

Deck 05 of the [Paul Wilmott Introduces Quantitative Finance &mdash; Companion Series](https://github.com/BrendanJamesLynskey/Wilmott_QF_Hub).

**Live presentation:** https://brendanjameslynskey.github.io/Wilmott_QF_05_Stochastic_Calculus/

A guided tour of chapter 5 of *Paul Wilmott Introduces Quantitative Finance*
(2nd edition, Wiley, 2007) &mdash; the machinery of Brownian motion, It&ocirc;
integration, SDEs and It&ocirc;'s lemma that the rest of the book assumes you
have internalised.

## What's inside

- Why classical calculus fails: unbounded variation of Brownian paths
- Markov and martingale properties
- Quadratic variation and the heuristic $(dW)^2 = dt$
- Brownian motion $W_t$ &mdash; the three defining properties
- The It&ocirc; integral, the left-endpoint rule, It&ocirc; isometry, martingale property
- Stochastic differential equations &mdash; $dX = a\,dt + b\,dW$ and the canonical zoo
- It&ocirc;'s lemma: $df = (f_t + a f_x + \tfrac{1}{2} b^2 f_{xx})\,dt + b f_x\,dW$
- Side-by-side comparison with the ordinary Taylor chain rule
- Worked examples: Brownian motion with drift, the lognormal random walk, the Ornstein&ndash;Uhlenbeck process
- **Interactive Brownian-path &amp; quadratic-variation visualiser** &mdash; sample paths, watch $\sum (\Delta W)^2$ converge to $T$, and see the histogram of $\Delta W$ converge to $\mathcal{N}(0, \delta t)$
